Challenge Invitation Templates for Friends

Planning and templates Oct 12, 2026 · By Quests

A useful challenge invitation tells people what you want to try, when it starts, how much time it needs and how to join. Include a clear reply question so a friend can accept, suggest a change or decline.

These are original example messages you can adapt for your own group. Replace every bracketed detail before sending. If you are still deciding whom to ask, start with the accountability partner guide.

Invite one reading partner

Want to try a short reading challenge together? I am planning [ten minutes] after [dinner] from [start date] to [finish date]. We can choose our own books and include audiobooks. I suggest a brief update in [place] after each session and a check-in on [review date]. Would that fit your week, or would a different target work better?

For example, Alex could send this to a friend who recently mentioned wanting to read more. The friend can choose five minutes while Alex chooses ten. Record those personal targets in the agreement once both people reply. The reading challenge guide covers books, listening and a sample first week.

Invite a small group

I am trying [five minutes of drawing] each day for [seven days] starting [Monday, date]. Anyone interested in joining? We will each choose a manageable target and share a short update in [place]. Sharing drawings is optional. We will review the round on [Sunday, date]. Reply by [date] if you want to help choose the details.

Use the reply deadline to organize the start. Give people a way to join the conversation before committing to the final rule. If the group chooses a different activity or duration, send the updated plan to everyone who expressed interest.

Invite an existing club

Would anyone in [club name] like a small practice round between our next two meetings? My suggestion is [action] on [days] from [date] to [date]. Participation is optional. We can agree on personal targets and one place for updates. I can bring a draft plan to [meeting]. Reply here if you want to discuss it.

Ask the organizer where an invitation belongs before posting in a moderated community. Keep ordinary club participation available to everyone. A challenge should have its own clearly stated commitment.

Send the final joining note

Our agreed activity:
Start and finish dates:
Each person's target:
Days we plan to practice:
Where to join or check in:
What an update includes:
Review date:
Who to contact about a change:

Download the invitation worksheet. Keep the final note short enough to find later. If your group chooses Quests, follow the first Quest guide and add the invitation link supplied by your chosen Quest.

Handle replies clearly

For a yes, confirm the next step. For a proposed change, ask the group to agree before editing the plan. For a decline, acknowledge it and leave the choice there. When someone has not replied, use only a follow-up they would reasonably welcome.

Once people join, the fifteen-minute kickoff agenda helps turn their replies into a shared plan.
