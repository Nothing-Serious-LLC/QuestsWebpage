FIVE QUESTIONS
1. What action did I plan, and on which days?
2. When did it fit naturally into my day?
3. What interrupted or prevented it?
4. Does the action still serve a reason I care about?
5. What one change will I try next week?

Keep planned rest days separate from missed opportunities.
Mark missing records as unknown. Share only what you choose to share.
