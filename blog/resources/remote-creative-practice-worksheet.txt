SIX OPTIONAL PROMPTS
1. Notice one ordinary object: sketch, describe or privately record it.
2. Make a small change to an earlier idea.
3. Collect three details and make a short scene or pattern.
4. Try one constraint, such as two colors or six lines.
5. Return to an earlier attempt and revise one detail.
6. Choose the next experiment you want to try.
Adapt each prompt for your interests, materials and access needs.
Keep confidential work and other people's identifying details out of sharing.
