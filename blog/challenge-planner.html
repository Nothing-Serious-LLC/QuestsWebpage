A little structure for a shared journey

Pick an action that fits your day. Connect it to a cue you recognize, agree on a comfortable check-in, and choose a review date. The suggested lengths give you a period to try the arrangement and learn from it.

The habit science guide explains research on planning, monitoring, context, and support. Your plan is a practical starting point. The right routine depends on your action, circumstances, and preferences.

Three ways to start

  • Read together: choose your own books, make time after a familiar daily cue, and share one interesting idea at the end of the week.
  • Make something: spend a few minutes sketching or writing, and decide whether you want to share the result.
  • Take a walk: choose a comfortable route and duration that suit each person, then compare how the routine fit your day.
