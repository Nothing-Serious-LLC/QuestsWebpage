What Is the Quests App? Habits With Friends, Explained

Product Sep 18, 2026 · Updated Oct 5, 2026

Quests is an app for building habits with friends through shared challenges. Choose something you want to do regularly, invite your people, and check in as you go. Your progress becomes part of a journey you can share.

The app is called Quests: Habits with Friends in the stores and is made by Nothing Serious LLC. This guide explains the daily experience and links to the official iOS and Android downloads.

What a Quest is

A Quest gives a habit a daily action and a finish line. You might read for ten minutes, practice drawing, or take a daily walk. Choose a target that fits your day and agree on what counts before you begin.

Explore existing challenges or create your own. Quests organizes challenges into five categories: Healthy, Mindful, Social, Productive, and Creative. For examples you can adapt, see our healthy challenge ideas.

How Quests works with friends

  1. Choose a challenge. Pick an existing Quest or create one around a habit you want to build.
  2. Invite your people. Share the Quest's invite link with friends who want to take part.
  3. Do the daily action and check in. Record your progress in the app. You can add a reflection or photo to your check-in.
  4. Follow the journey. See progress along the Quest's milestone path and encourage friends through reactions, comments, and Sparks.

A simple first challenge

Try seven days of reading with a friend. Agree to read for ten minutes each day, choose when you will check in, and decide how you will encourage each other if a day gets busy. At the end of the week, compare how the routine felt and choose whether to keep going.

This is one possible way to use Quests. Your group can choose its own action, duration, and pace. Our guide to starting a challenge with friends covers the decisions to make together.

Streaks, points, and rewards

Check-ins earn points, and streaks build as you keep showing up. Leaderboards give friends a way to follow each other's progress. You can also explore badges, avatar customization, and rewards in the app.

Available rewards and purchase options appear in Quests. Check the current offer details before choosing a subscription or redeeming points.

What the Quests app costs

Quests is free to download on iOS and Android, with optional in-app purchases and Quests Pro subscriptions. The app and your store listing show current prices, limits, and included features.

Your check-ins and privacy

Quests uses the information you provide to run your challenges and shared activity. Read the privacy policy and health data policy for details about how information is handled and the choices available to you.

For a walking or workout Quest, use your preferred device or fitness app to measure the activity, then record your check-in in Quests.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Quests app free?

Quests is free to download on iOS and Android. Check the app and your store listing for current optional purchases, subscriptions, and included features.

Do my friends need the app to join my Quest?

Yes. Each person uses Quests to take part. Share the Quest's invite link so your friends can find the app and join you.

Can I do a Quest alone?

You can use Quests for your own challenges and invite friends when you want to share the journey.

Does Quests track workouts or calories?

Quests lets you check in on the challenges you choose, such as a daily workout. You record your progress in Quests; detailed workout and calorie logging lives in your preferred fitness or nutrition app.
