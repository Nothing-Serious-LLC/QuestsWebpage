3.4 Contacts and invitations

Contacts are optional. You can use Quests without giving it access to your contacts. If you allow access, the app reads the names and phone numbers in your address book on your device to show you whom you can invite and which of your contacts already use Quests. To find matches, the app converts each phone number into a one-way hash (a scrambled code that cannot be turned back into the number) and sends only those hashes to our servers, where they are compared with the hashed numbers of existing users. We do not upload the names in your address book, and we do not keep a copy of it. We keep the hashes so we can tell you when one of your contacts joins Quests; they are deleted when you delete your account. You can withdraw contacts access at any time in your device settings.

Invitations. You send invitations yourself, from your own device, through your phone's share sheet or messaging app. We receive nothing about the recipient until they create an account.

Older versions of the app could send an invitation text on your behalf, from a Quests phone number, to a contact you selected. That feature has been retired in the current version. Where it was used, we stored the invited phone number for up to 14 days so that, if the person joined, we could add them to the quest; those numbers are deleted automatically after 14 days and are used for nothing else.

If you are not a Quests user and received an invitation text from a Quests number, we hold only your phone number, for up to 14 days, as described above. Reply STOP to the message, or write to nothingserious.team@gmail.com, and we will delete it immediately.

3.5 Habit data can be health-related

Some quests are about exercise, sleep, food, mood, medication, sobriety, or other things that can reveal information about your physical or mental health. When you do those quests, the quest content and your check-ins are health-related information. Section 6 and our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describe how we treat it. We do not integrate with Apple Health or Google Health Connect at this time; if we add that, we will ask for your permission first and will never send that data to any advertising or analytics partner.

3.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect precise geolocation. We do not upload the names in your address book (Section 3.4). We do not collect biometric identifiers. We do not collect government identification numbers except a taxpayer identification number on a Form W-9 when the law requires it (Section 3.1).
