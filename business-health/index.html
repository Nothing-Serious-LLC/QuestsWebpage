Quests Business Health

Quests Business Health is Nothing Serious LLC's private reporting tool for its authorized business operators.

It collects service-health and spending information, produces a compact daily business report, and emails that report to the authorized business mailbox. The owner reviews any alerts and decides what action to take.

Google account access

The tool uses Gmail Send and Gmail Read Only to send the report, prevent duplicate delivery, and verify that the matching report and attachment arrived in the owner's inbox. Google grants mailbox-wide read access. The implementation limits its searches and reads to its reporting and delivery checks.

Authorization is limited to the configured company mailbox. Operators can stop the local runner and revoke Google access at any time.

Read the privacy disclosure and terms of use before authorizing access.
