Quests Business Health terms of use

Quests Business Health is operated by Nothing Serious LLC solely for its authorized business operators. Use requires permission from the company and authorization for each connected service.

Purpose and review

The tool collects operational evidence and delivers a daily report. Provider data can be delayed or unavailable. Operators must review reporting windows, coverage labels, and source evidence when making business decisions. An unavailable source remains explicitly identified in the report.

Access and actions

Operators are responsible for granting approved access, keeping credentials private, and revoking access when it is no longer needed. Gmail permissions support sending to the configured company mailbox and reading back matching delivery evidence. The reporting process uses read-only access to business-service data.

Business changes, financial transactions, order changes, and messages to additional recipients require separate owner authorization.

Stopping access

The owner can stop the local runner, revoke connected-service permissions, and remove retained local files. The current pilot ends November 13, 2026. Continued operation requires a new owner decision.

The privacy disclosure explains the tool's Google data access, use, storage, and controls.
