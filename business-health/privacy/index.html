Quests Business Health privacy disclosure

Operator: Nothing Serious LLC. This disclosure covers the private Quests Business Health reporting tool.

Information accessed and purpose

The tool requests Gmail Send and Gmail Read Only for the authorized company mailbox. Google grants mailbox-wide read permission. The implementation checks the mailbox identity, sends business-health reports to that same configured mailbox, searches for matching report messages, and reads matching message bodies and HTML attachments. These checks deliver the requested report, prevent duplicate sends, reconcile uncertain sends, and verify receipt.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, source snapshots, generated reports, and delivery receipts are stored in files restricted to the owner's account on the authorized Mac. Credentials are kept separately from shared documentation and code exports. Report evidence is retained until the owner removes it. Copies sent through Gmail remain subject to the mailbox owner's retention settings.

Data use and sharing

Google account data is used solely for the authorized reporting and delivery functions. Access is limited to authorized operators and the services necessary to perform those functions. Additional disclosure requires the owner's consent or a legal obligation. Selling Google account data, using it for advertising, and using it to train generalized artificial-intelligence models are prohibited.

Quests Business Health's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Owner controls

The owner can stop the runner, revoke its access through Google Account settings, and delete local credentials and retained reports. Revoking access stops future API access; retained files and Gmail copies can be removed separately by the owner.

The current reporting pilot ends November 13, 2026. Continued operation requires a new owner decision.
